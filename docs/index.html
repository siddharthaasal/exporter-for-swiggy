Features

Everything you paid, line by line

Food and Instamart

One extension for both. Run it once on each site and get a separate file for each.

Any date range

Pick exact dates or a quick range. It stops reading the moment it passes your start date.

One tab per month

Plus a Summary tab with monthly totals, ready for budgeting, expense claims or taxes.

Fees broken out

Delivery, packing, platform, handling and surge fees, taxes, tips, coupons and discounts, each in its own column.

Bill details

On Instamart, optionally read each order’s full bill. Turn it off for a faster, lighter export.

Private by design

No server and no account. Your orders go from Swiggy to your spreadsheet and nowhere else.

How it works

Three clicks to a spreadsheet

  1. Open Swiggy

    Log in on swiggy.com or instamart.in and keep that tab active.

  2. Choose your dates

    Click the extension icon, set a range and decide whether to include cancelled orders.

  3. Export

    Watch the progress on the page. The .xlsx downloads when it finishes.

FAQ

Good to know

Does my data leave my browser?

No. The extension reads your orders from Swiggy’s own pages while you’re logged in, builds the spreadsheet on your device and saves it as a download. There’s no server, no account and no analytics.

Which sites does it work on?

Swiggy Food on swiggy.com and Instamart on instamart.in. Open whichever you want to export, then click the extension icon. It detects the site for you.

Can I export a specific period?

Yes. Pick any start and end date, or use Last month, This month and Last 3 months. It reads newest first and stops as soon as it passes your start date, so short ranges are quick.

What do I get?

One .xlsx file with a Summary tab and one tab per month. Every order is a row with the items, fees, discounts and what you actually paid.

What about cancelled orders?

Skipped by default. Untick “Delivered orders only” if you want them in the file too.

Why did an export fail?

The extension uses the same internal endpoints the sites’ order pages use. If Swiggy changes them, exports can break until the extension is updated. Make sure you’re logged in and the tab is on the right site.
