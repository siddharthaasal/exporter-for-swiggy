Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Swiggy & Instamart Order Exporter doesn’t collect, send or sell any of your data. Your orders are read in your browser and written to a file on your device.

What the extension does

When you click Export, the extension runs on the Swiggy or Instamart tab you’re logged in to. It requests your order history from the same endpoints those sites use for their own order pages, using your existing login, then builds an Excel file locally and downloads it.

What it handles

DataWhat happens
Your order historyRead from Swiggy or Instamart, processed in your browser and saved into the .xlsx you download.
Your loginThe sites’ own session is used. The extension never sees, stores or copies your password or cookies.
Your date choicesUsed for the export only. Nothing is saved.

What it doesn’t do

  • No accounts, servers, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party.
  • No remote code. The spreadsheet library is bundled in the extension.

Permissions

  • swiggy.com and instamart.in so it can read your orders on those sites, and nowhere else.
  • Scripting so it can run the export on the page you’re viewing when you click the button.

Your files

The exported spreadsheet contains your order details, so treat it like any financial record. Once it’s downloaded, it’s yours to store or delete.

Independence

This is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Swiggy or Instamart. Because it relies on the sites’ internal endpoints, it may stop working if they change.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
